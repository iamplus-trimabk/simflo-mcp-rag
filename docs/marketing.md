# SimFlo RAG - Marketing & Brand Guidelines

## 🧠 Core Tagline (Never Delete)

**"This is our living thought laboratory - ideas evolve here before becoming reality."**

## Brand Philosophy

SimFlo RAG embodies evolutionary development principles - we believe in the power of emergent design, iterative discovery, and letting great ideas evolve naturally through experimentation and learning.

## Key Messaging Points

- **Evolutionary Intelligence**: Systems that learn and adapt organically
- **Living Knowledge**: Dynamic RAG that evolves with your needs
- **Emergent Discovery**: Uncover insights through natural exploration
- **Adaptive Architecture**: Systems designed to evolve, not remain static

## Target Audience

- **Developers** seeking intelligent component discovery
- **Teams** practicing evolutionary development methodologies
- **Organizations** embracing adaptive, learning systems
- **Innovators** building the future of software development

## Voice & Tone

- **Thoughtful**: Deep consideration of complex problems
- **Evolutionary**: Embrace change, growth, and adaptation
- **Practical**: Focus on working solutions over theoretical perfection
- **Collaborative**: Build together through shared discovery
