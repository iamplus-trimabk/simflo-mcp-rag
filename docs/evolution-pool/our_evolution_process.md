## 🎯 Our Evolution Philosophy

### **Core Principles:**
- **Small Batches Only**: 2-3 tasks maximum at any time
- **Good Enough > Perfect**: Simple solutions that work
- **Data-Driven Evolution**: Let findings inform next steps
- **Working Software**: Focus on tangible progress
- **Emergent Design**: Architecture evolves naturally

### **Success Pattern:**
1. **Discuss** ideas and record immediately
2. **Plan** research based on hypotheses
3. **Build** simple tools to gather data
4. **Learn** from real results
5. **Evolve** with next 2-3 improvements
